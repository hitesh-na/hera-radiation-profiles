Final XGBoost models for recorded count rates

The pressure-only model gave the lowest average whole-flight test error for both HERA counter channels in the exploratory experiment. The final refit produced one XGBoost model per channel using 918 complete 30-second intervals from five flights. Each flight received equal total training weight.

The table summarizes earlier fits that each held out one complete flight. Scores were averaged across seeds 11, 42 and 73 within each flight, then across the five flights. These results guided the pressure-only selection. The delivered all-flight models use seed 42 and the same 180-tree settings. They still need an independent flight test.

Pfotzer peak screening

None of the 20 measured and predicted profile pairs qualified for a peak-location error at the primary setting of 250 m bins, at least three intervals per bin and three-bin smoothing. Sixteen pairs had too few supported bins. The other four had gaps in the supported altitude profile. Changing the grid to 100, 250 or 500 m and using one- or three-bin smoothing still yielded zero qualifying pairs. A peak altitude remains unresolved.

Training data and reproducibility

Flights used were July 20 and August 24, 2025, and March 28, May 31 and August 21, 2026. Pressure was the sole predictor. Both outputs are recorded counts per elapsed second. Detector live time and exact hardware settings for every flight remain unresolved.

All 5,508 pressure-only predictions from the 30 original held-out fits were reproduced exactly. Both saved final models reloaded with identical predictions. See the README for commands and the peak screening table for individual results.
